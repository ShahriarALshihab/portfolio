I learn faster when I explain things. So this blog is where I write down what I'm building,
what broke, and what finally fixed it.

## What to expect

- Short notes on projects I build
- Small lessons about TypeScript, React and Astro
- Mistakes, and what I did about them

> Writing is how I check that I really understood something.

If a post helps you, that's a bonus.
